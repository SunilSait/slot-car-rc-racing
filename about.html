Our Story

Built by Enthusiasts,
Engineered for Champions

Apex Raceway began with a singular obsession: to build a commercial-grade racing complex that honors the golden heritage of analog slot cars while pioneering the cutting edge of modern brushless RC competition.

What started in 2018 as a weekend gathering of scale motorsport mechanics has blossomed into the region’s premier indoor raceway. Our facility features custom CAD-routed wooden speedways, precision CRC FastTrack carpet circuits, infrared transponder timing down to 0.001 seconds, and a thriving community of over 850 active racers.

Safety & Rules

Safety Guidelines & Track Etiquette

We maintain rigorous standards to guarantee a safe, competitive, and enjoyable environment for all participants.

Battery Safety & Charging Protocols

All LiPo battery charging must take place inside flame-resistant LiPo charge bags at our designated pit charging stations. Never exceed recommended 1C/2C charging rates. Puffed or damaged packs are strictly prohibited.

Track Marshaling & Live Circuit Protocol

Only designated, certified corner marshals are permitted to step onto the active circuit to right overturned vehicles. Drivers must never enter the track boundaries during live heats. Emergency stop switches are located at every turn.

Vehicle Technical Inspection (Scrutineering)

Personal cars must pass a rapid safety tech check before entering competitive heats: ground clearance check (no chassis scratching track surface), secure battery strap, and protected pinion/spur gear covers.

Sportsmanship & Paddock Conduct

Apex Raceway is a family-friendly venue. Unsportsmanlike conduct, intentional ramming, or verbal abuse will result in heat disqualification. Controlled slow speed is strictly enforced when entering or leaving pit lanes.
