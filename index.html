Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about track rules, car rentals, lap timing, and private party bookings.

01 Do I need to bring my own slot car or RC car?
No equipment is required! We have a complete fleet of championship-spec slot cars and brushless 1:10 & 1:14 RC buggies available for rent with your track session. Experienced drivers are also welcome to bring and tune their personal chassis on our pro pit benches.
02 Can beginners and younger kids race without crashing?
Absolutely! Our digital powerbases feature adjustable voltage limiters (50%, 75%, 100%), allowing junior racers and beginners to race wheel-to-wheel at controlled speeds without de-slotting or damaging cars.
03 How does the live lap timing & scoring system work?
Every car on our track is fitted with an infrared optical transponder tracked down to 0.001-second precision. Results instantly stream to arena big screens and sync with your driver profile in our racer dashboard.
04 Do I need a reservation or are walk-ins welcome?
Walk-ins are always welcome based on lane availability! However, booking online in advance guarantees your preferred circuit, lane choice, and rental equipment during peak weekend hours and league nights.
