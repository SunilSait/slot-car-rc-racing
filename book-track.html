FAQ

Booking Questions Answered

Do I need to bring my own car?
No! We have a full fleet of rental cars available for all track types. You can also bring your own if it meets our class specifications.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
Yes. Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before your session. Reschedule anytime through your dashboard.
Is there a minimum age to race?
Slot car racing: minimum age 6. RC on-road and off-road: minimum age 8. Under 14s must be accompanied by an adult.
What is included in a rental session?
Your session includes track time, a rental car if needed, controller, safety briefing, and real-time lap timing access.
Can I book for a group or private event?
Absolutely! We cater for birthdays, corporate events, and club sessions. Contact us directly for group pricing and private hire options.
