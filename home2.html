Contact Support Book Session
01 Do I need to bring my own slot car or RC car?
No equipment is required! We maintain a complete fleet of championship-spec slot cars and brushless 1:10 & 1:14 RC buggies for rent with any session. You are also welcome to bring and tune your personal race cars.
02 Can children and beginners race without crashing?
Yes! Our digital slot track powerbases feature adjustable voltage limiters (50%, 75%, 100%), allowing junior racers and beginners to race side-by-side at safe, controllable speeds without de-slotting.
03 How do the real-time lap times & leaderboards work?
Every slot car and RC transponder is tracked by our overhead laser gate array down to 0.001 seconds. Results instantly stream to stadium arena displays and sync live with your personal racer profile dashboard.
